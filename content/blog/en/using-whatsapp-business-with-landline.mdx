Want to tap into the power of WhatsApp Business but don't want to use your mobile number? Good news! You can actually use your existing landline number. This opens up a world of possibilities for businesses that rely on landlines for their primary communication. Let's dive into how you can set it up.

## TL;DR

1.  Enter your landline number in the WhatsApp Business app.
2.  Wait for SMS verification to fail.
3.  Tap "Call Me" and answer the call to get your verification code.
4.  Enter the code to verify and start using WhatsApp Business!

## Step-by-Step: Connecting Your Landline to WhatsApp Business

Here's a simple, step-by-step guide to get you up and running:

1.  **Enter Your Landline Details:**
    *   Open the WhatsApp Business app on your smartphone.
    *   Select your country code from the list.
    *   Carefully enter your landline number, making sure to exclude the leading '0' (if applicable in your region).

2.  **The SMS Verification Wait:**
    *   Since landlines can't receive SMS messages, the app will attempt to send a verification code via SMS. This will inevitably fail, and that's perfectly okay! Just wait for about a minute.

3.  **"Call Me" to the Rescue!**
    *   After the SMS verification attempt times out, the magical "Call Me" option will appear. Tap on it!

4.  **Answer the Call & Note the Code:**
    *   Your landline will ring! Answer the call, and an automated recording will read out your verification code. Make sure you have a pen and paper handy to jot it down.

5.  **Enter the Code & Verify:**
    *   Head back to the WhatsApp Business app and carefully enter the verification code you received. This confirms that you have access to the landline number.

6.  **Welcome to WhatsApp Business!**
    *   Congratulations! You've successfully set up WhatsApp Business using your landline number. You can now start connecting with customers, setting up automated messages, and exploring all the features the app has to offer.
    *   **Bonus:** You can even send WhatsApp API messages through services like Ultramsg, expanding your communication capabilities even further!

## Important Note: Unlinking from Facebook

If your landline number is currently linked to a Facebook Business account or a WhatsApp seller platform, you'll need to unlink it first. This ensures that you can use the number directly within the WhatsApp Business app on your phone.
